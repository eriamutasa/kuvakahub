# KuvakaHub Independent Site Inspector Field Guide

This guide outlines the field operational procedures for Independent Site Inspectors conducting physical verification visits in Chinhoyi, Zimbabwe.

---

## 📱 Step 1: Receiving & Accepting Site Assignments
1. Log in to `/inspector/dashboard` on your smartphone.
2. Review assigned site visits detailing:
   - Project title & suburb location
   - Milestone scope (e.g. Footing Reinforcement & Concrete Pour)
   - Scheduled visit date
3. Click **"ACCEPT ASSIGNMENT"** to confirm your visit.

---

## 🔍 Step 2: Pre-Visit Submission Review
1. Open the inspection workbench at `/inspector/inspections/[milestoneId]`.
2. Review the read-only evidence submitted by the Builder (photos, videos, notes).
3. Note specific construction requirements (e.g. trench depth, steel mesh gauge, concrete mix rating).

---

## 🚧 Step 3: Physical Site Audit Protocol
1. Visit the site location independently in Chinhoyi.
2. Measure physical dimensions against project specs.
3. Inspect structural integrity (soil compaction, footing thickness, wall alignment, DPC installation).
4. Take original photos/videos directly on your smartphone.
5. *Critical Rule*: **Never reuse or re-upload photos provided by the Builder**. Your evidence must be independently captured on site.

---

## 📝 Step 4: Record Verification Report
On the Inspector Workbench:
1. **Upload Independent Evidence**: Attach your site photos/videos. (Stored in private `inspector-evidence` bucket; strictly invisible to the Provider).
2. **Summary Inspection Notes**: Record detailed engineering/technical notes. (Visible to Client & Admin only).
3. **Instructions for Builder (Provider Feedback)**: Enter clear, safe corrective instructions if work requires remediation. (Shared with Provider).
4. **Record Official Result**: Select one option:
   - `VERIFIED`: Work physically complete and conforming to specs.
   - `NEEDS_ATTENTION`: Minor corrective work required before approval.
   - `REJECTED`: Work non-conforming; major remediation required.
5. Click **"SUBMIT INSPECTION REPORT"**.

---

## ⚖️ Operational Authority Principles
- **Inspector role**: Independent physical verification authority.
- **Client role**: Final milestone approval authority.
- **Provider role**: Construction execution authority.
- **AI role**: No authority.
