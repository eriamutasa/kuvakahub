# KuvakaHub Diaspora Client Onboarding Guide

Welcome to **KuvakaHub** — built specifically for Zimbabweans living abroad who need to manage construction, renovations, and property repairs in Zimbabwe with full transparency and independent verification.

---

## 📌 Step 1: Create Your Client Account
1. Visit [https://kuvakahub.co.zw/register](https://kuvakahub.co.zw/register).
2. Select **"Diaspora / Property Owner"**.
3. Select your country of residence (e.g. United Kingdom, South Africa, United States).

---

## 📝 Step 2: Post a Construction Project
1. Click **"Post New Project"** from your Client Dashboard.
2. Select the trade category (e.g., *Builders & Masonry*).
3. Select the city and suburb in Zimbabwe (e.g., *Chinhoyi — Hunyani*).
4. Enter project title, detailed scope, budget estimate, and preferred timeframe.
5. Publish project to the marketplace.

---

## ⚖️ Step 3: Compare Quotations & Select Contractor
1. Review itemized quotes submitted by verified local Chinhoyi providers.
2. Compare **Labour vs Material breakdowns**, duration, and contractor ratings.
3. Click **"Select Provider"** to accept a quotation and activate the project.

---

## 🧱 Step 4: Set Milestones & Review Independent Site Verification
1. Structure project milestones (e.g., 1. Excavation $\rightarrow$ 2. Footings $\rightarrow$ 3. Box Brickwork $\rightarrow$ 4. Slab).
2. When the Builder finishes a milestone and submits photos, an independent site inspector is dispatched.
3. Open `/client/projects/[id]/milestones/[milestoneId]` to review:
   - **BUILDER CLAIMS** (Photos & notes uploaded by contractor)
   - **INDEPENDENT INSPECTION PROOF** (Photos, notes, and verification badge uploaded separately by inspector)
4. Click **"APPROVE MILESTONE"** once independently verified.

---

## 💵 Step 5: Payment Transparency
1. After approving a milestone, click **"APPROVE PAYMENT"**.
2. Transfer funds directly to the Provider off-platform using your chosen method (EcoCash, Bank Wire, InnBucks, Mukuru).
3. Click **"MARK AS PAID"** on KuvakaHub and enter the transaction reference.
4. *Important*: KuvakaHub records payment status for transparency; funds are transferred directly between Client and Provider.

---

## 🏆 Step 6: Complete Project & Submit Verified Review
1. Once all milestones are finished and approved, click **"MARK PROJECT COMPLETED"**.
2. Your permanent **KuvakaHub Construction Passport** is generated.
3. Submit a **Verified Provider Review** to help build trust for other diaspora property owners.
