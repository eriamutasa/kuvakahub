# KuvakaHub Provider Onboarding Guide (WhatsApp Shareable)

Welcome to **KuvakaHub** — Chinhoyi's construction and home-services marketplace. KuvakaHub connects local Zimbabwean builders, masons, plumbers, electricans, and contractors directly with diaspora clients seeking verified project work.

---

## 📲 Step 1: Create Your Account
1. Open [https://kuvakahub.co.zw/register](https://kuvakahub.co.zw/register) on your mobile browser.
2. Select **"Service Provider / Builder"**.
3. Enter your full name, mobile phone number (e.g. `0772998877`), email, and password.

---

## 🛠️ Step 2: Complete Your Professional Profile
1. Enter your business name (e.g., *Mukamuri & Sons Building Contractors*).
2. Select your trade category (e.g., *Builders & Masonry*, *Plumbers*, *Electricians*).
3. Set your operating area (e.g., *Chinhoyi — Hunyani, Cold Stream, Orange Groove*).
4. Upload photos of past work to your portfolio gallery.
5. Submit your profile for **Admin Verification**.

---

## 📋 Step 3: Discover Jobs & Submit Itemized Quotations
1. Check your Provider Dashboard or visit `/jobs`.
2. Review client project requirements in Chinhoyi.
3. Click **"Submit Quotation"**.
4. Separate your quote into clear itemized lines:
   - **Labour costs** (e.g., excavation, walling, slab pour)
   - **Material costs** (e.g., bricks, cement bags, sand, rebar)
5. Set estimated duration in days and proposed start date.

---

## 🏗️ Step 4: Work & Upload Progress Evidence
1. Once selected by a Client, open your project workspace at `/provider/projects/[id]`.
2. Click **"Start Work"** on milestone #1.
3. Take site progress photos or videos on your smartphone.
4. Click **"Upload Progress Evidence"** and attach your files.
5. When work for a milestone is complete, click **"Submit Milestone for Inspection"**.

---

## 💳 Step 5: Receive Payment & Confirm Receipt
1. The Client pays you directly off-platform using your agreed payment method (EcoCash, Bank Wire, InnBucks, Cash).
2. Once the Client records the payment as paid on KuvakaHub, you will receive a notification.
3. Open your project workspace and click **"CONFIRM RECEIPT"**.

---

## ❓ Need Assistance?
Contact KuvakaHub Support on WhatsApp or use the **"Send Feedback"** button inside the app.
